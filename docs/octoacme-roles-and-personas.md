# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers help teams deliver reliably by aligning technical execution with product priorities, team capacity, and quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Forecast team capacity and delivery throughput
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on scope, sequencing, and dependencies
- Support technical planning, design reviews, and risk reduction
- Coordinate work allocation and staffing across team members
- Help resolve delivery bottlenecks and escalate issues when needed

### Goals
- Improve predictability of delivery
- Maintain a sustainable engineering pace
- Ensure technical work supports business goals and quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and engineering capacity review
- Delivery health updates with PMs and product leads
- Technical dependency discussions and escalation paths

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to support execution quality and coaching
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Partners with Project Managers to align roadmap commitments, milestones, and risks

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
The QA Lead defines quality gates, test strategy, release-readiness checks, and the validation approach needed to reduce delivery risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans, quality criteria, and release readiness standards
- Coordinate regression, integration, and smoke-test coverage
- Prioritize bug triage and validation with engineering and product teams
- Confirm that acceptance criteria are measurable and testable
- Escalate quality risks that could affect launch or customer experience

### Goals
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Improve confidence in release quality and user experience
- Align quality criteria with business and product expectations

### Typical Communication
- QA planning and release gate reviews
- Defect triage with engineering and product stakeholders
- Testing status updates during sprint reviews and release checks

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works directly with Developers to validate fixes and test coverage
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria and user-impact expectations
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release readiness and risk communication

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Partner helps protect the project from policy, privacy, and technical security risks while making sure delivery work aligns with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review security and compliance requirements for the project
- Identify technical and process risks related to data, access, and environment controls
- Support secure design reviews and remediation planning
- Coordinate incident reporting and follow-up when policy or security issues arise
- Advise on risk acceptance, exceptions, and mitigation plans

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Make risk decisions visible and actionable
- Enable safe, compliant delivery without unnecessary delays

### Typical Communication
- Risk review meetings and design checkpoints
- Security updates during sprint or milestone reviews
- Escalation for critical vulnerabilities or compliance concerns

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to assess secure implementation and remediation needs
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk trade-offs and escalation paths
- Supports stakeholder communications around security impact and governance requirements

---

## Technical Writer / Docs Owner

### Role Summary
The Technical Writer / Docs Owner ensures that project knowledge, release communication, and operational guidance stay accurate, accessible, and usable for the team and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project documentation, handoff notes, and operational runbooks
- Draft release notes, onboarding guides, and process updates
- Translate technical changes into clear internal and stakeholder-facing language
- Support adoption and documentation quality across the delivery lifecycle
- Coordinate with contributors to keep documentation aligned with delivery changes

### Goals
- Reduce knowledge gaps and onboarding friction
- Improve clarity and consistency of project communications
- Capture institutional knowledge as the project evolves

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews and content updates
- Release communication and stakeholder briefings
- Knowledge-sharing sessions and process clarifications

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to validate technical accuracy and explain system changes
- Supports Project Managers and Product Managers with concise status and release communication
- Helps maintain the project docs that support training, onboarding, and future continuity

---

## Customer Success / Support Lead

### Role Summary
The Customer Success / Support Lead represents the user and support perspective, helping the project team understand adoption, customer impact, and post-release feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Surface customer pain points, support issues, and escalation trends
- Partner with product and delivery teams on rollout communication and service impact
- Define readiness for launch based on customer experience and operational support needs
- Share feedback from frontline teams into planning and backlog refinement
- Support issue prioritization based on customer impact and urgency

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Maintain a clear line of feedback from users to delivery teams
- Reduce avoidable support escalations after launch

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback loops and support issue reviews
- Launch readiness and stakeholder status updates
- Cross-functional prioritization of urgent user-impact issues

### Interaction with existing roles
- Informs Product Managers with user insights, adoption barriers, and support trends
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release communication and mitigation planning
- Works with Developers to clarify customer-impact issues and verify fixes

---

## Data / Insights Analyst

### Role Summary
The Data / Insights Analyst helps the team measure whether the work is creating value by tracking relevant signals, outcomes, and operational trends.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track success metrics, adoption indicators, and product health signals
- Analyze usage, quality, and operational data to inform decisions
- Provide evidence for go/no-go decisions, releases, and iteration priorities
- Partner with product and delivery leads on impact evaluation and retrospectives
- Surface risks and trends that may require action before they become larger issues

### Goals
- Improve decision-making with data-informed evidence
- Measure actual project outcomes versus goals
- Support continuous learning and iteration after launch

### Typical Communication
- Metric reviews and KPI dashboards
- Launch or milestone impact summaries
- Retrospective analysis on project performance and customer effect

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Product Managers with evidence for prioritization and metric validation
- Helps Project Managers monitor progress, risks, and release outcomes
- Works with Developers and QA teams to connect technical performance to user and business impact

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles help clarify accountability across planning, execution, quality, communication, and release management.
